ReplyQuest Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 31, 2026

ReplyQuest is a browser extension that helps users draft context-aware replies for supported social platforms, video comments, chat pages, and webmail reply threads.

This Privacy Policy explains what information ReplyQuest processes, how that information is used, when it is shared with service providers, and what choices users have.

Information We Collect and Process

Account and Identity Information

  • Email address
  • Google-authenticated user identity
  • Supabase user ID
  • Account plan status
  • Trial start and end dates
  • Subscription status and billing entitlement identifiers

Authentication Information

ReplyQuest stores authentication session data locally to keep users signed in and verify account access.

Website Content and Personal Communications

  • Social media posts
  • Comments and replies
  • YouTube titles, descriptions, comments and transcripts
  • Visible Discord message context
  • Email thread content used for replies
  • Other visible text required to generate a response

Generated Reply Content

Relevant context and selected tone information may be sent to AI services to generate responses.

Usage and Entitlement Data

  • User ID
  • Platform where replies are generated
  • Daily usage counts
  • Plan and entitlement status
  • Trial or subscription status

Payment Information

Payments are processed by Dodo Payments. ReplyQuest does not collect or store full credit card details.

How We Use Information

  • Generate AI-powered replies
  • Recommend reply tones
  • Authenticate users
  • Maintain active sessions
  • Manage subscriptions and trials
  • Enforce usage limits
  • Improve reliability and security
  • Prevent abuse and troubleshoot issues

AI Processing

ReplyQuest uses Anthropic's API to analyze visible context and generate replies. Context may include posts, comments, chats, transcripts, or email thread content required for reply generation.

ReplyQuest does not intentionally store source context or generated replies in its own application database after processing is complete.

Local Browser Storage

  • Supabase session data
  • Access and refresh tokens
  • Signed-in account status
  • Trial and entitlement indicators
  • Usage and notification flags

Clipboard Access

ReplyQuest may write generated replies to the clipboard when direct insertion into an editor fails. It does not read clipboard contents.

Information Sharing

Anthropic

Processes prompt content required for AI reply generation.

Supabase

Provides authentication, storage, usage tracking, and account services.

Google

Used exclusively for Google Sign-In functionality.

Dodo Payments

Processes subscriptions, checkout, and billing-related events.

Legal and Safety

Information may be disclosed when required by law or to protect users, the service, or the public.

What We Do Not Do

  • Sell user data
  • Use data for unrelated advertising
  • Determine creditworthiness
  • Read clipboard contents
  • Collect full payment card information
  • Collect precise location data intentionally
  • Collect full browsing history intentionally
  • Log keystrokes or mouse movements intentionally

Security

ReplyQuest uses HTTPS communication, authenticated requests, browser-based session storage, and server-side protections to safeguard user information.

User Choices and Controls

  • Sign in or remain signed out
  • Edit generated replies before posting
  • Sign out of the extension
  • Uninstall at any time
  • Revoke Google access
  • Request account deletion or data access

Children's Privacy

ReplyQuest is not intended for children under 13 years of age or users below the minimum age required by applicable law.

International Users

Information may be processed by service providers in different countries where privacy laws may differ from those in your location.

Changes to This Policy

ReplyQuest may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The effective date will be updated whenever material changes occur.

Contact

For privacy questions, account deletion requests, data access requests, or support inquiries, contact the ReplyQuest owner using the support contact information provided where this policy is published.
