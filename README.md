# ReplyQuest Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** May 31, 2026

ReplyQuest is a browser extension that helps users draft context-aware replies for supported social platforms, video comments, chat pages, and webmail reply threads. This Privacy Policy explains what information ReplyQuest processes, how that information is used, when it is shared with service providers, and what choices users have.

This policy applies to the ReplyQuest Chrome extension.

## Information We Collect and Process

ReplyQuest only processes information needed to provide AI reply generation, Google sign-in, account status, trial limits, Pro subscription access, and billing-related entitlement features.

### Account and Identity Information

When you sign in with Google, ReplyQuest may process:

- Email address
- Google-authenticated user identity
- Supabase user ID
- Account plan status, such as trial, free, or Pro
- Trial start and end dates
- Subscription status and related billing entitlement identifiers

ReplyQuest uses Google sign-in through Supabase Auth. ReplyQuest does not collect or store your Google password.

### Authentication Information

To keep you signed in, ReplyQuest stores authentication session data locally in the browser extension. This may include Supabase access tokens and refresh tokens. These tokens are used to verify your account, refresh your session, check your plan, and apply usage limits.

### Website Content and Personal Communications

To generate a useful reply, ReplyQuest may read and process the visible context around the reply box on supported websites. Depending on where you use the extension, this may include:

- Social media posts
- Comments and replies
- YouTube video titles, descriptions, comments, and available transcript text
- Recent visible Discord message context
- Existing email thread text in supported webmail reply flows
- Other visible text needed to understand the reply context

ReplyQuest is designed for reply flows. It does not intentionally inject into brand-new email compose windows where no previous thread context is available.

### Generated Reply Content

When you ask ReplyQuest to generate or recommend a reply, the extension sends the relevant visible context and selected tone information to the AI generation service. The AI service returns a generated reply, which ReplyQuest inserts into the active editor or copies to your clipboard if direct insertion fails.

### Usage and Entitlement Data

ReplyQuest may record limited usage information required for quota, trial, and Pro-plan enforcement, such as:

- User ID
- Supported platform where a reply was generated
- Daily reply usage count
- Plan and entitlement status
- Trial or subscription status

ReplyQuest does not use this information to build a browsing profile or sell advertising.

### Payment and Subscription Information

Payments are handled by Dodo Payments. ReplyQuest may receive and store limited subscription information needed to activate or manage your Pro plan, such as:

- Dodo customer ID
- Dodo subscription ID
- Subscription status
- Plan start and end dates

ReplyQuest does not collect or store full credit card numbers, card security codes, or complete payment instrument details. Payment details are handled by Dodo Payments according to its own policies.

## How We Use Information

ReplyQuest uses the information described above to:

- Generate AI replies for supported websites
- Recommend an appropriate reply tone
- Insert generated replies into the active reply editor
- Copy generated replies to the clipboard when insertion fails
- Authenticate users with Google through Supabase
- Keep users signed in
- Check trial, free, or Pro entitlement status
- Enforce free-plan and trial usage limits
- Open checkout for Pro upgrades
- Maintain subscription status after payment events
- Prevent abuse and troubleshoot account or service issues
- Improve reliability and security of the extension

ReplyQuest does not use user data for unrelated advertising, creditworthiness, lending decisions, or sale to data brokers.

## AI Processing

ReplyQuest uses an AI service to generate replies and recommend tones. When you use ReplyQuest to analyze context or generate a reply, the relevant prompt data may be sent to Anthropic's API. This may include the visible post, comment, chat, video, transcript, or email-thread context needed to draft the reply.

ReplyQuest does not intentionally store the source context or generated reply text in its own application database after the reply-generation request is completed. Third-party service providers may process request data according to their own terms, privacy policies, and retention practices.

## Local Browser Storage

ReplyQuest uses Chrome extension storage to keep the extension working across supported pages. Local storage may include:

- Supabase session data
- Access and refresh tokens
- Signed-in account status
- Trial or entitlement display flags
- Usage or notice flags, such as whether a trial-ended notice has already been shown

You can clear this local data by signing out where available, removing the extension, or clearing extension and site data through your browser settings.

## Clipboard Access

ReplyQuest requests clipboard write permission only to copy a generated reply when direct insertion into a website's editor fails. ReplyQuest does not read your clipboard contents.

## Host Permissions

ReplyQuest requests host permissions for supported platforms and services so it can provide its single purpose: drafting context-aware replies.

These permissions allow ReplyQuest to:

- Display reply controls on supported websites
- Read visible reply context needed to generate a response
- Insert generated replies into the active editor
- Access Anthropic for AI generation
- Access Supabase for authentication, account, quota, and checkout functions

ReplyQuest does not use host permissions to collect full browsing history.

## Information Sharing

ReplyQuest shares information only as needed to operate the extension.

### Anthropic

Anthropic receives prompt content needed to recommend tones and generate replies. This may include visible website content or personal communication context from supported reply flows.

### Supabase

Supabase provides authentication, account storage, profile data, usage tracking, trial status, and server-side functions used by ReplyQuest.

### Google

Google is used for sign-in. Google may process authentication information when you choose to sign in with your Google account.

### Dodo Payments

Dodo Payments processes checkout and payment transactions. ReplyQuest may send or receive limited data needed to create checkout sessions and update subscription status, such as your email address, user ID, product information, customer ID, subscription ID, and plan status.

### Legal and Safety

ReplyQuest may disclose information if required by law, legal process, security investigation, or to protect the rights, safety, and integrity of ReplyQuest, users, or the public.

## What We Do Not Do

ReplyQuest does not:

- Sell user data
- Use user data for unrelated advertising
- Use user data to determine creditworthiness
- Use user data for lending decisions
- Read clipboard contents
- Collect full credit card details
- Intentionally collect health information
- Intentionally collect precise location data
- Intentionally collect full browsing history
- Intentionally log keystrokes, mouse movements, or scroll behavior

## Data Retention

ReplyQuest keeps account, entitlement, subscription, and usage information for as long as needed to provide the service, enforce plan limits, maintain billing records, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and protect against abuse.

Authentication tokens stored locally remain in the browser extension until they expire, are refreshed, are removed during sign-out, or are removed when the extension data is cleared.

ReplyQuest does not intentionally store source website context or generated reply text in its own application database after reply generation. Third-party providers may retain data according to their own policies.

## Security

ReplyQuest uses reasonable technical and organizational safeguards to protect user information. These include HTTPS-based API communication, authenticated requests, local browser storage for extension session data, and server-side handling for subscription and webhook events.

No system can guarantee absolute security. Users should keep their browser, operating system, and Google account secure.

## User Choices and Controls

You can control your use of ReplyQuest in several ways:

- You can choose whether to sign in with Google.
- You can choose whether to click ReplyQuest controls on supported pages.
- You can edit or delete generated replies before sending them.
- You can sign out of ReplyQuest where available.
- You can uninstall the extension at any time.
- You can revoke Google account access through your Google account settings.
- You can contact us to request account deletion or data access.

## Account Deletion and Data Requests

To request deletion of your ReplyQuest account data, access to your data, correction of inaccurate data, or other privacy-related help, contact the ReplyQuest owner at the support contact listed wherever this policy is published.

ReplyQuest may need to verify your identity before processing certain requests.

## Children's Privacy

ReplyQuest is not intended for children under 13 years old or for users below the age required by applicable law to use online services. ReplyQuest does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## International Users

ReplyQuest may process information through service providers located in different countries. By using ReplyQuest, you understand that your information may be processed in countries where privacy laws may differ from those in your location.

## Changes to This Policy

ReplyQuest may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If material changes are made, the effective date will be updated.

## Contact

For privacy questions, data requests, or support, contact the ReplyQuest owner at the support contact listed wherever this policy is published.
